Ecstatic Dance

We hold Ecstatic Dance sessions about every 2–3 weeks, depending on the weather; if it rains, the event is canceled.

The dance takes place outdoors on our beautiful ceremonial grounds, surrounded by ancient giant trees with a fire in the center.
​
The music is a mix of medicine music, electronic music, and classical music;
it’s always a little journey that takes you through a range of emotions.
​
Don’t worry—there are no dance steps, and you don’t need to know how to dance.
Just move however you feel like, and maybe you’ll reach a point where your body shows you movements that are completely new to you.

It’s simply about enjoying the music, the movement, and nature.

Space & Rules

To ensure everyone feels comfortable, there are a few simple ground rules that are generally followed in Conscious Dance:

01

We talk to each other before and after. During the dance, we try not to talk, simply to be more in our bodies and less in our heads.

02

It is a safe space. No one should feel observed—it is not a club where you stand on the sidelines and watch.
You can move, step aside, or lie down—just be careful not to stare intently at others.

03

Respect others’ space. Emotions may arise, or someone may simply want to be alone.
Interaction is possible—always with respect and empathy.
Pay attention to the signs if someone doesn’t want contact right now.

And don’t worry—this might sound stricter than it is. As a rule, it’s simply easy, beautiful, and often very fun. The rules just help us relax better together.

About the Session

Please arrive a few minutes early so you can settle in at your own pace.
We’ll sit together around the fire at the beginning and end to share our experiences.
Water will be provided. Feel free to bring your own water bottle and warm clothes for later.
